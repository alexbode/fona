Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic registration information such as your email address when you sign up. We also keep track of your training progress, including completed chorusing sessions and minimal pair exercise scores. If you use our voice feedback tool, audio data is processed locally on your device to generate waveforms and is not stored permanently on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to provide, personalize, and improve your training experience on Fona. This includes displaying your performance history, managing subscriptions, and tailoring future accent training recommendations. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Service Providers

We use trusted infrastructure services like Supabase to handle user authentication, database management, and progress storage. These service providers access your data only to perform tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

4. Data Security

The security of your personal data is important to us. We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and secure database rules, to protect your credentials and training history.

5. Your Choices & Deletion

You have control over your data. If you wish to delete your account or wipe your progress history, please reach out to us. We will delete all of your personal details and training progress from our databases in a timely manner.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices, or wish to make requests regarding your data, please contact us by creating an issue on our GitHub repository.
